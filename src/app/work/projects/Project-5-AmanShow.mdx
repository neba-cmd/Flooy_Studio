## Overview
Habesha View is a prominent global media and entertainment platform dedicated to Ethiopian and Eritrean cultural content. When the network expanded its event footprint into the UK, my media practice, Flooy Studio, was selected as their official London production partner. In this role, We took complete ownership of capturing live high-profile events, filming key promotional highlights, and producing engaging short-form assets to boost digital event traction.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Official Production Partnership**: Positioned Flooy Studio as the trusted on-the-ground media team for Habesha View's London-based events and promotional tours.
- **Event Cinematography**: Captured high-energy cultural gatherings, stage performances, VIP arrivals, and behind-the-scenes moments under dynamic live event conditions.
- **Promotional Content Creation**: Developed, filmed, and quickly edited short-form recaps and teasers deployed across social channels to drive platform engagement and future ticket sales.
- **Brand Alignment**: Collaborated directly with the Habesha View team to ensure all visual assets perfectly aligned with the network’s premium broadcast standards and commercial identity.

## Outcome
The partnership successfully elevated Habesha View's digital footprint during their UK runs. The high-retention video recaps generated significant social media engagement and strengthened their community ties within the UK diaspora, cementing a reliable production framework for future international events.
