## Overview
For over a year and a half, we managed the social media presence for Crafty Fox Market, a market events brand, growing their audience through consistent, high-quality short-form video content. We owned the full content pipeline from idea to final edit across Instagram Reels.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Content Ideation**: Generated weekly content ideas tailored to market events, vendor highlights and behind-the-scenes moments.
- **Filming**: Directed and filmed Reels on location at markets and events, capturing both planned shots and spontaneous moments.
- **Editing**: Edited all footage into polished, platform-optimised Reels designed to maximise reach and engagement.
- **Audience Growth**: Maintained a consistent posting cadence and content style that grew the account's following and engagement over time.

## Outcome
The Crafty Fox Market account saw sustained growth in followers and engagement throughout my time managing it, with Reels becoming a key driver of brand visibility for the market.
