## Overview
We've been with Dream Arts for over 2 years, taking on the role of social media manager while also introducing filmmaking into the organisation's creative output. We produced original short films to accompany Dream Arts REP's theatre productions, bringing a new visual storytelling element to their live performances.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Social Meida Managment**: Managede the digital presence of the company for multi-year period.
- **Filmmaking for Theatre**: Wrote, filmed and edited short films designed to accompany and enhance live theatre productions for Dream Arts REP.
- **Digital Presence**: Helped shape Dream Arts' communications strategy and online presence across social platforms.

## Outcome
The short films produced for Dream Arts REP became a regular feature alongside their theatre productions.
