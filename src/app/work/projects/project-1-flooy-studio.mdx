## Overview
Flooy Studio is our own videography and photography practice, where we work directly with couples, families and businesses to capture life's biggest moments. Since launching, We've filmed and photographed more than 10 weddings, alongside numerous christenings, birthdays and private events handling everything from initial client conversations to final delivery.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Client Liaison**: Met with couples and families ahead of each event to understand their vision, plan the day's coverage, and set expectations.
- **Filming & Photography**: Captured ceremonies, speeches, candid moments and portraits across full wedding days and shorter celebration events.
- **Editing & Delivery**: Edited highlight films, full ceremony footage and photo galleries, balancing storytelling with quick turnaround times for clients.
- **End-to-End Ownership**: Managed every stage of the process from booking and shooting to colour grading and final hand-off.

## Outcome
Flooy Studio has built a track record of reliable, story driven coverage across weddings, christenings and birthdays, with client work spanning over a year of continuous bookings. The studio continues to grow through referrals and repeat clients.
