## Overview
Afropod is a podcast platform dedicated to deep-dive community conversations and social commentary[cite: 1]. Flooy Studio stepped in as the production partner to overhaul their visual layout and manage their rapid, weekly broadcast schedule[cite: 1]. We successfully structured an efficient, repeatable technical framework to film, record, and edit full-length studio episodes without compromising on visual or audio fidelity[cite: 1].

## Key Responsibilities
- **Weekly Multi-Cam Production**: Managed on-set multi-camera configurations to smoothly track multiple hosts and guests simultaneously[cite: 1].
- **Audio Engineering**: Engineered clean studio audio tracking, minimizing ambient noise and handling level balancing for broadcast clarity[cite: 1].
- **Post-Production Delivery**: Edited raw recordings on a strict weekly turnaround—handling multi-cam cutting, sound mastering, color correction, and intro/outro graphic placement[cite: 1].
- **Asset Optimization**: Sliced full-length episodes into high-engagement vertical clips optimized for distribution on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels[cite: 1].

## Outcome
Through our ongoing production partnership, Afropod has built an organized, high-quality video archive on YouTube and established a polished visual identity[cite: 1]. The reliable content pipeline has allowed the show's creators to focus entirely on booking high-value guests and driving community discussions, while Flooy Studio handles the entire technical engine[cite: 1].
